Ptarmigan Ridge Run

September 2026 | 1 day | 11 miles | +2384' | 

Caltopo Link

This trail is probably the GOAT in terms of low effort high reward. The parking lot is literally worth the drive. It's about as close as you can get to Baker without climbing it.

I have hiked this trail twice before and been almost entirely socked in both times. Third time's a charm.

This was my third day hiking in a row. I planned on taking it easy but once I got going I ended up running almost all of it.

The trail climbs gently over 5 miles to a place called "The Portals".

Views at the terminus.

I was thrilled to finally do some volcano basking this year.

Glacier Peak in the distance. Ranier was faintly visible also but not enough for a photo.

So ended my "Canada" trip.
