Our second Oregon coast trip, this time with van and dog.

Our new favorite hobby is dispersed camping. Free, first come first serve campsites on public lands. Most of the time we are using iOverlander to find these sites or at least find areas to start looking for them.

We did not pay for lodging at all on this trip, or plug into power, or get water from a spigot. We cooked all of our own meals except one, and some coffees on the road. We had internet from our hotspot most of the time, and plenty of power from our solar setup. We used our portable shower and public restrooms for cleanliness, and dug cat holes in the backcountry.

45.95958, -123.54877

The first destination was mostly chosen to be as far away from fireworks noise as possible for 4th of July weekend, since they give our dog panic attacks. We started at a cluster of sites in a logging region 45.81776, -123.50779, but had to relocate due to target shooting in a nearby valley, which also triggers our dog. We ended up in a similar type of location 30 minutes away, where we found a super quiet and isolated spot with a great view.

We spent 3.5 days here, heard no fireworks and saw one other vehicle the entire time. Also had a great internet connection. The weather was fair, hot in the afternoons but with a strong breeze that flowed through van like a wind tunnel with all the doors open.
