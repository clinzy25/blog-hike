Grand Pass & Elk Mountain

August 2026 | 2 days | 15 miles | +5680' | 

Caltopo Link

Since scheduling trips in the the Cascades continued to be a logistical battle this season due to the fires and odd weekend weather, I decided to hop over to the Pinnensula to do some trail running on the Olympic Grand Route. I am not badass enough to crank the entire route yet, but I did get to run some of the best parts of it on this trip.

I have been to the Olympics countless times but somehow had not been up to Hurricane Ridge yet. For this trip I base camped at Obstruction Point in my camper van and did a couple of out and back day runs. Oobstruction Point is a sick place to go in general as it is a little further from the crowds on Hurricane Ridge and drops you immediately into high country. Probably one of the lowest effort, highest reward places in the state.

Day 2

I looked east from the parking lot to the end of the Grand Route (if you do it CCW). Target was Elk Mountain, an easy day with some time at the end to check out Hurricane Ridge.

The actual trail traversed below the ridge, but it was very simple to ascend it run the ridge. I opted for this, turning the day into a loop instead of and out and back. The ridge was mostly hiking with a couple of light scramble spots. Did my best to avoid meadow stomping as there was not much of a trail.

View of the Strait of Juan de Fuca. On a clear day you could likely see Victora, BC from here.

Returned back to the van here and the drove up to join the throbbing mobs at Hurricane Ridge. I walked up Hurricane hill and back down, thankful that there are still swaths of untouched places on the Pinnensula.
