Mount St. Helens via Worm Flows

October 2026 | 1 day | 10.2 miles | +5872' | 

Caltopo Link

My first Cascades volcano. I intended to start from Sno-Park, which was closed, so I went to the next access point at June Lake trailhead. I was completely unaware of the existence of the Climber's Biovouac before I drove past the turn-off for it, even though I was, at the time, literally a climber looking for a bivvy. Sometimes I simply cannot explain myself or the things that I do.

Why the Worm Flows you ask, which is traditionally a winter route? See above.

I kicked this one off at 6am after sleeping in a turn-off. Short hike up to June Lake and then west across a boulder field to meet up with the Worm Flows route.

Mount Jefferson is the first to appear.

The climbing was pretty bad. Mix of volcanic ash, dirt, and large boulders. Not technical but a bit of a slog, and required a lot of attention.

Great views of Adams.

Finally met up with the main route at around 7600ft.

Mountain turns into sand/ash dune.

Shortly after I arrive at the crater rim. This is not the summit, which lies 0.4 miles to the west.

Now heading toward the summit.
