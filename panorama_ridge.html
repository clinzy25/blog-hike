September 2026 | 1 day | 17 miles | +5245' | 

Caltopo Link

I started my week-long break with a Washington / BC itenerary, but in true 2026 fashion, the weather was not cooperating in WA. I spent the first few days of this trip in the North Cascades, putzing around the Mount Baker area anticipating a weather window to climb Tomyhoi Peak. When it became clear that it wasn't going to be clear (huehue), I rage quit the entire state of Washington and built an epic Canada itenerary, all the way out to the Canadian Rockies. I lined up three long-standing bucket list items - Garibaldi Provencial, Glacier, and Yoho National Park.

This was my first time in CA as an adult. I drove the 99 along the Howe sound, suprised by the reliefs and how much bigger the mountains seemed than Washington just a hundred miles south. I stopped in Squamish to admire the Yosemite-esque big walls surrounding the town, and made an effort locate bear spray. Couldn't find any so figured I'd just fight any grizzlies I encountered.

I had been trying to get out to Garibaldi to hike for 3 years, so I was pretty stoked. After spending the night on a non-descript forest road off the 99, I headed to the trailhead at around 6:30am. It immediately became apparent I would be fighting the weather, and was anticipating no visibility until the afternoon, so I took my time climbing up through the moist, mossy and misty PNW forest.

The clouds were clearing when I reached Taylor Meadows.
