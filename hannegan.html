Hannegan Peak

September 2026 | 1 day | 11 miles | +3413' | 

Caltopo Link

Hannegan Peak is such a good mountain. It was the first WA peak I ever climbed back in 2022 when I hiked the Copper Ridge. The trail starts as deep in the N Cascades, as far east as you can go if you start on the Baker Hwy (there is no mountain pass here).

The trail meanders up the Ruth Creek Valley for several miles before reaching Hanngan Pass.

Baker and Shuksan are the highlights here.

Easy walk to the summit.

Picket Range visible.

The remote Chilliwacks containing 5 Bulger Peaks.

Recently learned of Slesse in Canada. Insane relief.

Ruth Mountain that I contemplated tackling but was too worked from the day before.

Here are a couple of pictures from July 2022 from the same spot.

In the evening, I drove up to this place called "The Knoll", which is a kick ass dispersed campsite beneath Shucksan. Someone else had claimed it for the night, he was super chill and we hung out for while.
